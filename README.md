# HR Analytics - PowerBI Rescue Story

**Turning employee data into actionable HR insights through data analytics and strategic decision-making.**

## Project Overview

*HR Analytics Rescue Story* is a story-driven HR analytics case study that explores how data analysis can help an organization investigate rising employee attrition, identify workforce risks, and develop targeted retention strategies.

The project presents an HR crisis as a business problem and follows the analytical journey from identifying warning signs to proposing data-driven interventions.

## Business Problem

An organization faces a sudden employee attrition crisis, creating risks for workforce stability, employee engagement, and critical client deliverables.

The key questions are:

- Which workforce segments face the highest attrition risk?
- How is overtime associated with employee turnover?
- Are employees leaving during their early tenure?
- Which departments require targeted retention strategies?
- How can HR use analytics to support timely decisions?

## Dataset Overview

- **Employee records:** 1,470, as presented in the case study
- **Analysis focus:** Attrition, overtime, department-level patterns, and employee tenure
- **Objective:** Identify risk areas and translate findings into actionable HR strategies

## Tools & Technologies

## Analytical Approach

1. **Problem Identification:** Recognize the attrition crisis and its potential business impact.
2. **Data Exploration:** Investigate employee records and workforce patterns.
3. **Risk Identification:** Examine overtime, early-tenure turnover, and department-level risks.
4. **Strategy Development:** Propose targeted retention measures.
5. **Business Impact:** Present the potential value of retention initiatives and predictive analytics.

## Key Findings Presented in the Case Study

- **16.1% attrition rate:** The initial attrition level highlighted in the case study.
- **Overtime risk:** The analysis narrative reports substantially higher attrition among employees working overtime.
- **Early-tenure turnover:** The case study highlights a potential retention problem among employees in their first two years.
- **Department-level risk:** R&D and Technology are identified as priority areas for intervention.
- **Retention strategy:** Proposed actions include overtime controls, structured career development, stay interviews, and predictive analytics.

## Proposed Business Recommendations

- Monitor overtime and establish appropriate workload thresholds.
- Strengthen career development for early-tenure employees.
- Conduct regular stay interviews to understand employee concerns.
- Use workforce analytics to identify emerging attrition risks.
- Support HR decisions with measurable KPIs and management dashboards.
